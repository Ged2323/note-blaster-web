Note Blaster

Hears you play. Point your phone at your guitar, pluck the note on screen, and it fires — detected from the real sound of your guitar, not a button tap.

Features

  • NEW — Mic mode: play real notes on a real guitar, the app hears you
  • Acoustic mode for quiet, unplugged guitars in noisy rooms
  • Learn step by step, one string at a time
  • Practice area to study notes before you play
  • Three difficulty modes: Easy, Intermediate, and Hard
  • Session Mode — a teacher picks the notes, a student plays a mini-game, results save automatically
  • Session Scoreboard — every student's practice history at a glance
  • Local high score leaderboard

Note Blaster turns learning the fretboard into something you actually want to play.
