Note Blaster

Learn guitar notes by playing them. Point your phone at your guitar, pluck the note on screen, and it fires — detected from the real sound of your guitar, or from a USB MIDI keyboard or MIDI guitar.

Features

  • Mic mode: play real notes on a real guitar — the app hears you, and ignores voices and room noise
  • MIDI mode: plug in a USB MIDI keyboard or MIDI guitar, with a live connection light and level meter
  • HOW TO READ guide: tap any fret to see where that note sits on the staff, with Every Good Banana Drives Ferraris and FACE memory aids
  • Acoustic mode for quiet, unplugged guitars in noisy rooms
  • Learn step by step, one string at a time
  • Practice area to study notes before you play
  • Three difficulty modes: Easy, Intermediate, and Hard
  • Session Mode — a teacher picks the notes, a student plays a mini-game, results save automatically
  • Session Scoreboard — every student's practice history at a glance
  • Local high score leaderboard

Note Blaster turns learning the fretboard into something you actually want to play. Built by a working guitar teacher, for the practice room.
